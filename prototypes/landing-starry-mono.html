Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

A cloud storage service that fetches files from the internet at blazing speed, then lets you stream them anywhere.

↧ --fetch <url>
Transfer Engine

Drop a link — any link. Torrents, magnet links, direct URLs. We fetch it to your cloud storage at up to 1 Gbps. No client software needed.

▶ --stream
Media Player

Stream any video format instantly. Subtitles auto-detected. Chromecast, AirPlay, Apple TV, Android TV, Roku — pick your screen.

🔒 --private
Security

Your files are yours. End-to-end encryption, no data selling, no ads, no tracking. We've been privacy-first since 2011.

🌐 --everywhere
Cross-Platform

iOS, Android, Apple TV, Android TV, Roku, web, desktop, CLI. Access your files from literally anywhere on any device.
