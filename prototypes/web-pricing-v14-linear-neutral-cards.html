Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account settings. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle, and you'll be credited for any unused time.
What happens to my files if I downgrade?
Your files stay available for 30 days after downgrading. During that time you can download anything you need. After 30 days, files exceeding your new storage limit will be removed.
Do you offer refunds?
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact support and we'll process your refund — no questions asked.
Is there a Turkey discount?
Yes. Regional pricing is auto-detected based on your location. If you're in Turkey, discounted pricing will be shown automatically at checkout.
