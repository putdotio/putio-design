Privacy Policy

We believe privacy isn't a feature — it's a right. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with your data.

Contents

i.

General

put.io is committed to protecting your privacy. We're a small team in Istanbul. We don't mine data. We don't sell your information. We don't build profiles on you.

We make money from subscriptions, not your information. That's been true since 2011 and it's not changing.

ii.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We update this policy occasionally. When we make significant changes, we'll notify you — by email, by in-app notification, or both.

We won't sneak anything past you. No dark patterns, no buried clauses. If something changes that affects how your data is handled, you'll know about it.

iii.

How Your Information is Used

3.1 Account & Service

We collect your email address and payment information to provide the service. That's it. Emails we send are transactional only — account confirmations, payment receipts, service notices. Nothing you didn't ask for.

3.2 Marketing

We will never send you marketing emails without your explicit consent. If you opt in and change your mind, the unsubscribe link actually works — immediately, not "within 10 business days."

3.3 Service Improvement

We use anonymous, aggregated data to improve the service. We don't track what you download. We don't track what you store. Your files are your business.

iv.

Government Disclosure

We comply with valid Turkish legal requests only. We are incorporated in Turkey and operate under Turkish law.

If we receive a legal request for your data, we will notify you unless we are legally prohibited from doing so. We don't volunteer information to any government, anywhere.

v.

Cookies & Tracking

5.1 Essential Cookies

We use essential session cookies only — the kind that keep you logged in and make the site work. No cookie banners needed because there's nothing shady to consent to.

5.2 Analytics

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how our service is used in aggregate. No Google Analytics. No tools that build advertising profiles from your browsing behavior.

5.3 Third-Party Trackers

Zero. No Facebook pixels. No remarketing tags. No ad network scripts. No cross-site tracking of any kind. When you leave put.io, we don't follow you around the internet.

vi.

Third-Party Links

Our service may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. Once you leave put.io, their rules apply, not ours.

vii.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers in the European Union.

When you delete a file, it's deleted. Not "marked for deletion" — actually deleted. When you delete your account, everything is gone. Your files, your metadata, your account information. No shadow copies. No 90-day retention periods. Gone.
