Hello, We're pilli

A 26 year old web company hailing from Istanbul.

We built put.io because we believe the internet should be faster and more private.

Our Story
How we got here
2000
The beginning
A web design shop in Istanbul. Young, hungry, building sites for anyone who'd pay.
2005
Our own products
We stopped making things for other people. PilliNetwork was born -- our first attempt at building something we actually wanted to use.
2007
Sosyomat
Social media monitoring before it was a phrase anyone used. 400,000 users at its peak. We learned what scale felt like.
2011
put.io
Cloud storage that downloads things for you. What if your files lived in the cloud and it was really fast? The one that stuck.
2013
put.io v2
Complete rewrite. Better streaming, better everything. We learned that sometimes you have to throw it all away and start over.
2026
Now
26 years. 7 people. No VC. No exit strategy. Just building something useful from Istanbul.
What We Believe
Values
Utility is Beautiful
The product's soul comes from making file operations feel premium. Every interaction should feel considered, fast, and respectful of your time.
Respect the Power User
Default to simplicity. Power features exist but don't clutter. The surface is calm; the depth is there for those who look.
Trust Through Craft
Premium design communicates implicit trust. Craft communicates legitimacy. When every pixel is intentional, people feel safe.
