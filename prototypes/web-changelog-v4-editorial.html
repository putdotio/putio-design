New Feature

RSS Automation

your shows, on autopilot

Here's the thing about watching TV shows through put.io: you had to remember to go check if a new episode was out, find it, add it, wait for the transfer, then watch it. Every. Single. Week.

Not anymore. Set up an RSS feed for any show and put.io takes over from there. New episodes get fetched, downloaded, and neatly organized into the right folder. You wake up, the episode's waiting. It's beautiful.

"Set it up once. Never think about it again.
That's the whole pitch."

New Feature

Storage Dashboard

see what's taking space

We've all been there. "Where did my 50 terabytes go?" Well, now you can actually see. The new Storage Dashboard breaks down your usage by file type, age, and size -- with a cleanup wizard that makes it absurdly easy to reclaim space.

Found 200 GB of subtitle files you downloaded in 2019? Gone. That Linux ISO you "needed" three years ago? See ya. Reclaim your storage without the archaeology expedition.

Improved

Family Sharing

now with up to 4 members

Family Sharing just got a serious upgrade. You can now add up to 4 members to your put.io account. Each person gets their own private space, their own transfer queue, and their own watch history. No more "who downloaded this?" detective work.

One subscription, the whole crew. Everyone's happy. Except maybe your bandwidth provider.

Invite Your Family
New Feature

Browser Extension

one click from any page

Right-click any link on any webpage and send it straight to put.io. That's it. That's the feature. It works on Chrome, Firefox, and Edge.

The toolbar icon shows your active transfers in a neat little dropdown, so you can keep an eye on things without switching tabs. It's the kind of feature that makes you wonder how you ever lived without it.
