Hello, We're pilli

A small, passionate team building the best way to store, organize, and stream your content from the cloud.

(*^_^*)/

put.io started in 2013 with a simple idea: your files should be accessible from anywhere, instantly. No waiting. No hassle. Just your stuff, ready when you are.

We're a bootstrapped, profitable company based in Istanbul. No venture capital. No growth-at-all-costs. Just a sustainable business built by people who love what they do.

Our Journey

How We Got Here SINCE 2013

2013
put.io is Born
Launched as a cloud storage service focused on fetching and streaming content. The first version was built in a tiny apartment in Istanbul.
2015
Mobile Goes Live
Released iOS and Android apps, bringing put.io to phones and tablets everywhere. Streaming on the go becomes a reality.
2018
Living Room Era
Apple TV and Android TV apps launch. Your content now plays on the biggest screen in the house.
2022
1 Million Users
Reached 1 million registered users worldwide. Still bootstrapped, still independent, still having fun.
2026
The Next Chapter
Redesigning everything from the ground up. New apps, new ideas, same commitment to making your digital life easier.
