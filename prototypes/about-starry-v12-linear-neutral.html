Hello, We're pilli

Our Story
How we got here
2000
Started as a web design shop
Young, hungry, building sites for anyone who'd pay. Istanbul was our home, the web was our playground.
2005
Built our own products
We stopped making things for other people and started building what we actually wanted to use.
2008
PilliNetwork
Community blogs, the Spotify model before Spotify. Creators owned their space, audiences found them organically.
2009
Sosyomat
Social tagging platform. 400,000 users at its peak. We learned what scale felt like.
2011
put.io launched
Cloud storage that downloads things for you. What if your files lived in the cloud and it was really fast? The one that stuck.
2026
Still here
Still 7 people. Still Istanbul. No VC. No exit strategy. Just building something useful.
What We Believe
Values
Utility is Beautiful
The product's soul comes from making file operations feel premium. Every interaction should feel considered, fast, and respectful of your time.
Respect the Power User
Default to simplicity. Power features exist but don't clutter. The surface is calm; the depth is there for those who look.
Trust Through Craft
Premium design communicates implicit trust. Craft communicates legitimacy. When every pixel is intentional, people feel safe.
