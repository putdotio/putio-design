Hello, We're pilli.

A 26 year old web company hailing from Istanbul.

Makers of put.io since 2011

We started as a tiny web design shop in Istanbul back in 2000. A few friends who loved the internet when it was still weird. We made websites for other people, but what we really wanted was to build our own things.

So we did. Over 26 years, we shipped products that made us laugh, made us think, and sometimes even made us money. put.io is the one that stuck.

Our Story

How we got here

2000
The beginning
A web design shop in Istanbul. Young, hungry, building sites for anyone who'd pay. The internet felt like it belonged to all of us.
2005
Our own products
We stopped making things for other people and started making things for ourselves. PilliNetwork was born -- a platform for creative communities.
2007
Sosyomat
Social media monitoring before "social media monitoring" was a phrase anyone used. We were early. Maybe too early.
2011
put.io
Cloud storage that downloads things for you. The idea was simple: what if your files lived in the cloud and the cloud was really, really fast? Turns out people liked that idea.
2013
put.io v2
Complete rewrite. Better streaming, better everything. We learned that v2 is where you figure out what you actually are.
2024
Still here, still building
15 years of put.io. Apps on every platform. A small team that ships fast and argues about fonts. We wouldn't have it any other way.
2026
Now
26 years as a company. ~6 people. No VC. No exit strategy. Just building something useful for people who love the internet as much as we do.
