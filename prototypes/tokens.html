Icon Disambiguation

The #1 UX problem from user research: download, save, and stream all look the same. These three actions must be visually distinct everywhere.

Save to put.io
Cloud + upload arrow
Adds to your cloud storage
Download to Device
Device + down arrow
Saves to your phone/computer
Play / Stream
Play triangle
Stream immediately from cloud
