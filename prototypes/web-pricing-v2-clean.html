Questions you'd actually ask

What happens when I hit my storage limit?
Nothing dramatic. You can still stream everything you have. You just can't add new stuff until you make room or upgrade. We'll nudge you, not punish you.
Can I switch plans anytime?
Yep. Upgrade instantly, downgrade at the end of your billing cycle. We prorate everything, because we're not monsters.
What does "availability period" mean?
After your file is downloaded, it stays in the cloud for that many days. After that, it's cleaned up to free space. You can always re-download.
