Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how we collect, use, and protect your personal information -- explained in plain language.

Effective date: March 1, 2026

Information We Collect How We Use It Sharing & Disclosure Data Storage & Security Cookies & Tracking Your Rights Children's Privacy Changes & Contact
1

Information We Collect

When you create an account or use put.io, we collect certain information to provide and improve our services. This includes:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and payment details when you sign up or subscribe to a plan.
  • Usage data: how you interact with our service, including features used, transfer history, storage usage, and session duration.
  • Device information: your IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers when you access put.io.
  • Files and content: the files you store on put.io, including metadata such as file names, sizes, and types. We do not access the contents of your files unless required by law or to maintain our service.

We only collect what we need. If you'd rather not share optional information, you can skip it -- the core service will still work just fine.

2

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Providing the service: processing your transfers, managing your storage, enabling streaming and downloads across your devices.
  • Account management: handling billing, authentication, and customer support requests.
  • Improving put.io: understanding usage patterns to make our product faster, more reliable, and more intuitive.
  • Communication: sending service-related emails such as account confirmations, billing receipts, security alerts, and (with your consent) product updates.
  • Safety and compliance: detecting abuse, preventing fraud, and complying with legal obligations.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties. That's a promise, not a marketing line.

3

Sharing & Disclosure

We share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted third parties who help us operate put.io, such as payment processors, cloud infrastructure providers, and analytics services. They only access what they need and are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process (such as a court order or subpoena).
  • Safety: to protect the rights, property, or safety of put.io, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not share your file contents with any third party unless compelled by law.

4

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on servers located in the European Union and the United States. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: all data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Access controls: strict internal access policies ensure only authorized personnel can access user data, and only when necessary.
  • Infrastructure: our servers are hosted in SOC 2 compliant data centers with physical security, redundancy, and 24/7 monitoring.
  • Retention: we retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data and files within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe and will notify you promptly if a breach occurs that affects your information.

5

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how you use put.io.

  • Essential cookies: required for the service to function -- authentication, session management, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand usage patterns and improve the product. You can opt out of these at any time.
  • No advertising cookies: we do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell data to ad networks. Period.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or by contacting us at privacy@put.io. Most browsers also offer a "Do Not Track" signal, which we respect.

6

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: request that we delete your personal data and account.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format so you can transfer it elsewhere.
  • Objection: object to certain types of processing, such as analytics or marketing communications.
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.

If you're in the EU, these rights are protected under the GDPR. If you're in California, you have additional rights under the CCPA. To exercise any of these rights, reach out to privacy@put.io -- we'll respond within 30 days.

7

Children's Privacy

put.io is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will delete that information promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at privacy@put.io so we can take appropriate action.

8

Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we'll notify you by email or through a prominent notice on our website at least 30 days before the changes take effect.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this policy or your personal data, you can reach us at:

  • Email: privacy@put.io
  • Address: put.io, Istanbul, Turkey

We're real people on the other end, and we're happy to help. Don't hesitate to get in touch.
