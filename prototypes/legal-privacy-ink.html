Privacy Policy

Effective: March 19, 2026

1.
Introduction

Put.io Ltd. ("put.io", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our cloud storage and file retrieval service, including our website, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service immediately.

2.
Information We Collect
2.1

Account Information. When you create an account, we collect your email address, username, and password. If you choose to subscribe to a paid plan, we collect billing information through our third-party payment processor. We do not store complete credit card numbers on our servers.

2.2

Usage Data. We automatically collect information about how you interact with the Service, including IP addresses, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, links clicked, and the date and time of your visits.

2.3

File Metadata. When you use the Service to download or store files, we collect metadata such as file names, file sizes, file types, and transfer timestamps. We do not monitor or analyze the content of files you store.

2.4

Device Information. We collect information about the devices you use to access the Service, including device identifiers, operating system versions, and application versions, to optimize performance and troubleshoot issues.

3.
How We Use Your Information
3.1

Service Delivery. We use your information to provide, maintain, and improve the Service, including processing your file transfers, managing your account, and delivering customer support.

3.2

Communication. We may use your email address to send you service-related notices, including confirmations, technical notices, updates, security alerts, and administrative messages. You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

3.3

Analytics and Improvement. We use aggregated, anonymized usage data to understand how users interact with the Service, to identify trends, and to improve functionality, performance, and user experience.

3.4

Legal Compliance. We may use your information as required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or enforceable governmental requests.

4.
Data Sharing and Disclosure
4.1

Service Providers. We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as payment processing, data hosting, analytics, and customer support. These providers are contractually obligated to use your data only as directed by us.

4.2

Legal Requirements. We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect our rights or property, prevent fraud, or ensure the safety of our users.

4.3

Business Transfers. In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change in ownership or control of your personal data.

4.4

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5.
Data Storage and Security
5.1

Your data is stored on servers located in the European Union. We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments.

5.2

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to promptly notifying affected users in the event of a data breach as required by applicable law.

5.3

Files you store through the Service are retained for the duration of your account. Upon account deletion, your files and associated metadata are permanently removed from our systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6.
Your Rights
6.1

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. You may also have the right to restrict or object to certain processing activities, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

6.2

European Economic Area residents have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion.

6.3

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@put.io. We will respond to your request within 30 days, or as required by applicable law. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

7.
Cookies and Tracking
7.1

We use essential cookies to enable core functionality such as authentication and session management. These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to operate and cannot be disabled.

7.2

We use analytics cookies to understand how users interact with the Service. These cookies collect anonymized data and help us improve performance and usability. You may opt out of analytics cookies through your browser settings or our cookie preference controls.

7.3

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking technologies for the purpose of targeted advertising. We do not participate in cross-site tracking or share cookie data with advertising networks.

8.
Changes and Contact
8.1

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email or by posting a prominent notice on the Service prior to the changes becoming effective. Your continued use of the Service after any modification constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

8.2

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@put.io. Our data protection officer can be reached at dpo@put.io.
