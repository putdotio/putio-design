Our story

We started in Istanbul in 2011

We had a simple idea: what if cloud storage could fetch things for you? Instead of uploading files from your slow home connection, what if the cloud went out and grabbed them at datacenter speed?

Fifteen years later, that's still what we do. No pivot. No "AI-powered" buzzwords. Just really, really fast cloud storage that fetches content and lets you stream it everywhere.

We're a small team. We care about the product. We think that shows.
