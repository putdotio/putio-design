01

General

put.io ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our service.

We're a small team in Istanbul. We don't have a data mining department because we don't mine data. We make money from subscriptions, not from selling your information.

02

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the date at the top and notify you via email if the changes are significant. We won't sneak anything past you.

03

How Your Information is Used

3.1 Account & Service

We collect your email address and payment information to provide the service. That's it. We use your email to send transactional messages (receipts, password resets, security alerts) and nothing else unless you opt in.

3.2 Marketing

We will never send you marketing emails without your explicit consent. If you do opt in, every email has an unsubscribe link that actually works.

3.3 Service Improvement

We collect anonymous, aggregated usage data to improve the product. We don't track what you download, stream, or store. Your files are your business.

04

Disclosure to Government

We comply with valid legal requests from Turkish authorities. We will notify you of any request for your data unless legally prohibited from doing so. We have never received a national security letter or FISA order.

05

Cookies & Tracking

5.1 Essential Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. These are strictly necessary and cannot be disabled.

5.2 Analytics

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how people use our website (not our app). We don't use Google Analytics. We don't track you across the web.

5.3 No Third-Party Trackers

We don't embed Facebook pixels, Google remarketing tags, or any other advertising trackers. Zero.

06

Third-Party Links

Our service may contain links to external sites. We're not responsible for their privacy practices. Click at your own risk (but you knew that).

07

Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers in the European Union. When you delete a file, it's deleted. When you delete your account, everything goes. We don't keep shadow copies.

08

Contact Us

Questions? Email support@put.io or visit put.io/help. We're real humans, not a ticket queue.
