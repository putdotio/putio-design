What We Collect

We only collect the information we genuinely need to make put.io work for you. Nothing more, nothing sneaky.

We will never sell your personal data to anyone. Period. Full stop. That's a promise.

Account information:

  • Email address and password (hashed, of course)
  • Username and display name
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment provider)
  • OAuth tokens if you sign in with a third-party account

Usage information:

  • IP address and approximate location (country level)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Feature usage patterns and interaction data
  • Transfer and download activity metadata

We do not scan, index, or analyze the contents of your files. Your files are your business.

2

How We Use It

Every piece of data we collect has a clear, specific purpose. Here's exactly what we do with it:

  • Run your account -- authenticate you, manage your subscription, process payments
  • Provide the service -- download your files, manage your storage, deliver your streams
  • Improve the product -- understand how features are used so we can make them better
  • Communicate with you -- send service updates, respond to support requests, occasional product news
  • Stay secure -- detect fraud, prevent abuse, protect the platform for everyone
We don't use your data for targeted advertising. We never have, and we never will.

We may create anonymized, aggregated data from usage patterns for statistical analysis. This data cannot be linked back to you in any way.

3

Sharing & Third Parties

We are extremely selective about who gets access to any of your information. Here's the full picture:

Service providers we work with:

  • Payment processors (to handle billing securely)
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (to host and deliver your files)
  • Analytics tools (anonymized usage data only)
  • Customer support platforms (to help you when you need it)
All our service providers are contractually bound to protect your data and can only use it for the specific service they provide to us.

We may also share information when required by law, to protect our rights, or to prevent harm. If we ever receive a government request for your data, we will notify you unless legally prohibited from doing so.

4

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on servers located in the European Union and the United States. We take security seriously -- here's how we protect your information:

  • All data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2+ (that's the lock icon in your browser)
  • Files at rest are encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt -- we literally cannot read them
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Strict access controls -- only authorized personnel can access infrastructure
We retain your data only as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your files and personal data are permanently removed within 30 days.

If we ever experience a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you within 72 hours, as required by GDPR.

5

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies, but only the ones that actually matter. Here's our full cookie menu:

Essential cookies (always on):

  • Session cookies to keep you logged in
  • Security cookies to prevent cross-site request forgery
  • Preference cookies to remember your settings

Analytics cookies (opt-in):

  • Anonymous usage analytics to understand how people use put.io
  • Performance monitoring to keep things fast
We don't use any third-party advertising cookies. No tracking pixels. No retargeting. Your browsing habits are none of our business.

You can manage cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings or our cookie management panel in your account settings.

6

Your Rights

You have full control over your data. Regardless of where you live, we extend these rights to all our users:

  • Access -- request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction -- fix any inaccurate information in your account
  • Deletion -- delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability -- export your data in a standard, machine-readable format
  • Objection -- opt out of specific data processing activities
  • Restriction -- limit how we process your data in certain circumstances
To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@put.io. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

If you're in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We hope it doesn't come to that, but we respect the process.

7

Children's Privacy

put.io is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16 years of age.

If we discover that we've collected data from a child under 16, we will delete it immediately. No questions asked.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@put.io and we will take immediate action to remove that information from our systems.

  • We update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • For significant changes, we'll notify you by email
  • For major changes that affect your rights, we'll ask for your explicit consent

We encourage you to check back occasionally, but we'll always make sure you know about the important stuff.
