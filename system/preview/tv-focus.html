Focus — two voices, one accent
Row focus is calm — soft glass pill with a 1px yellow halo. Big surfaces (file rows, settings rows, picker items) stay calm because there are many of them on screen at once. Chrome focus is loud — small things (action pills, nav tabs, segmented controls) invert to a luminous white pill so they shout at three metres. Lift values from tv-shell.css.
