Player chrome — minimal, bottom-anchored
Apple TV owns the player. Minimal overlay: bold filename bottom-left, two icon circles top-right, hairline scrubber in the bottom safe area, GT America timecodes with tabular figures. No transport buttons — the Siri Remote owns play/pause/scrub. Android TV, Roku, and Smart-TV web mirror this shape, but their apps draw the chrome because those platforms don't ship a system player.

This is the one TV surface outside our focus contract. The focused CC circle inverts to white because that is Apple's focus idiom and Apple draws this chrome — not ours. Everything put.io draws focuses with the --component-bg-active fill and never a white invert — rows take that fill alone, while bordered chrome (buttons, keys) also steps --border to --border-hover. On the platforms that do draw their own player, use the standard treatment. Source: tv-shell.css.
