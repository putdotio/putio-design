Phone welcome: the signed-out screen on iOS and Android
Both apps sign in through put.io OAuth in the browser and have no password form or sign-up link. Sign in opens the browser: ASWebAuthenticationSession on iOS, an Auth Tab on Android.
Proposed: neither app ships it yet. iOS ships a 240pt wordmark and one Log in button; Android ships one still kaomoji over Welcome!.
Kaomoji take turns over a mono yellow caption, with page dots under them. Each beat holds 2s and rolls up in 0.6s, then the loop restarts. Reduced motion holds the first beat.
