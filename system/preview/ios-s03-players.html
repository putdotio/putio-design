Do not ship a custom video player on Apple platforms. If a feature is missing from AVKit, a stream-quality switch, a subtitle offset, surface it as a menu item on the system player or as a route in the file's context menu.

Rebuilding the transport to add one control loses PiP, the Lock Screen, CarPlay and the remote.
