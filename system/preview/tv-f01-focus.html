Focus, a fill, never a lift
TV focus is one treatment everywhere: the surface fills with --component-bg-active. No scale, no shadow, no halo, no white-invert. Rows take the fill alone; chrome (header action buttons) takes the fill and steps its border from --border to --border-hover, because a button already carries a border at rest.

Label and icon stay --text in both states. Source: list-item.tsx · button.tsx.

Platform variation · implementation, not redesign
The tier-4 visual for tv.put.io, Tizen and webOS comes from the same React Native list-item.tsx and button.tsx. Roku follows the same fill-and-border family through SceneGraph. Android TV is a separate tier-2 Compose presentation: the product anatomy and tokens carry across, while Compose owns focus scale and elevation.

In the tier-4 implementation, focus applies instantly. pressable.tsx is a bare re-export of RN Pressable: no Animated, no timing curve, and tvParallaxProperties sets enabled: false.
