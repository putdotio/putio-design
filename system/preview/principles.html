Design principles.

five rules · governs everything downstream
Principle 01foundation

Utility is beautiful

The product’s soul comes from making file operations feel premium — not from decoration. A clean transfer row earns more love than any hero illustration.
Principle 02copy & iconography

Clarity over cleverness

Every icon, button, and label has exactly one meaning. Download means to-this-device. Save means to-put.io. Stream means play-without-download. Never mix.
Principle 03typography

Type carries the weight

We don’t have posters, hero art, or metadata to lean on — a filename is the content. GT America does the heavy lifting; mono fonts handle numerics and identifiers.
Principle 04cross-platform

One look across platforms

What changes between web, mobile, and TV is the engine — focus model, blur support, player ownership. The visual stays put.io.
Principle 05brand & restraint

Voice & restraint

Brand-yellow #FDCE45 is sacred — used for focus, brand moments, and one CTA at a time. Kaomoji stays. Everything else is a variable: subtract before you add.
