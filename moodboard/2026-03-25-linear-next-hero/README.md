## Interaction

3D mouse-tracking rotation on the Linear app icon in the hero section. The logo is rendered with a dithered/stippled black-and-white aesthetic (no smooth gradients — just dots and diagonal lines). As the cursor moves, the icon rotates in 3D space, revealing depth and curvature.

## Key Traits

- **3D cursor tracking** — logo rotates to follow mouse position in real-time
- **Dithered rendering** — halftone/stipple texture gives a retro-print feel
- **Subtle depth** — the sphere surface and diagonal stripes shift with perspective
- **Dark background** — high contrast, minimal surrounding chrome
